About Lunara

How it all started — and where we’re going.

🌙 How Lunara Really Started

Lunara didn’t begin with a business plan or a polished pitch deck. It started with a spark — and a lot of chaos.

Arthur had an idea: build something creative, something alive. He reached out to Abhi, and together they started small — just a minigame, no big team, no fancy setup. Just two people, ambition, and late-night energy.

Then came the name. Arthur chose Lunara because of the moon — its quiet rhythm, its mystery, its power. That feeling became the identity. He added a tagline that stuck:

🌌 Lunara: See Beyond. Build Forward.

At first, it was about games. But Arthur had one of those corridor thoughts — the kind that hits when you’re walking alone and suddenly everything makes sense. Why stop at games? What if Lunara was a space where anyone could create — code, design, edit, build, share, and grow?

So Arthur and Abhi kept building. No guarantees, no perfect plan. Just persistence. Slowly, Lunara became more than a name. It became a place where creativity could breathe.

We’re still figuring it out. Still growing. But we’re here. And we’re moving. Thanks for being part of this.

Lunara’s goal: to be the all-in-one app for creative community, music, games, tech, and more.
